Source contract from Gridiron's own methodology

Mission Control treats Gridiron as a probabilistic external model, not a binary pick service. The contract below is based on the methodology/FAQ screenshots supplied in chat.

1 · Project

Gridiron says its models estimate game/player outcomes from current and historical NFL data, including probabilities, scoring estimates, margins and player projections.

2 · Compare

Available odds/lines are compared with model estimates so the size and direction of a potential difference from market are visible.

3 · Grade

Eligible published selections are graded after games. Mission Control likewise preserves the pregame snapshot and grades it later rather than rewriting the rationale.

What “edge” means

Gridiron defines edge as the difference between model probability and the probability implied by the betting price. Mission Control therefore displays a Gridiron edge only when both values are captured for the same market reference.

Rule: model probability − market-implied probability.

Update / editorial behavior

Gridiron says predictions update when weekly model data refreshes and should be rechecked close to kickoff as injuries, roster changes and market movement evolve. It also states explanatory content does not manually reorder the model projections.

Missing is not neutral. If SportsLine, Gridiron, M1–M5, or another component is unavailable at a known reference line, it receives no score and no vote. We do not impute a 50% probability or treat absence as disagreement.

Mistake attribution taxonomy

Every losing wager—or winning wager with bad process—gets one primary process label only after the postgame audit. Labels describe the failure mode; they are not excuses for the result.

MODELPrediction error

Frozen predictive evidence favored the wrong side even though the data and price were correctly captured.

PRICENumber-selection error

We took a materially worse spread or crossed a key number without sufficient compensation.

NEWS / DATAInformation error

A relevant status/source was missing, stale, misread, or arrived before lock and was not incorporated.

RED TEAMContradiction-handling error

A material objection was identified but underweighted, or a weak objection vetoed a genuinely robust edge.

SIZINGPortfolio error

The side may have been reasonable, but exposure/duplication was too large relative to evidence and bankroll.

NONE / VARIANCEGood process, bad result

Price, evidence, news handling, and sizing all pass audit. The loss is recorded without inventing a corrective lesson.

Promotion guardrails

MINIMUM HISTORYMulti-week only

No production change can be promoted from a single week or a single source hot streak.

OUT-OF-SAMPLELeave-one-week-out

A candidate must improve results when each completed week is held out in turn, not merely fit the weeks that created it.

SHRINKAGESmall changes first

Any future production reweight is shrunk toward the current baseline and capped at a small step per review.

MULTIPLE SCORECARDSNo single-metric winner

Promotion requires improvement in probability calibration / selection quality without materially degrading price quality or portfolio risk.
